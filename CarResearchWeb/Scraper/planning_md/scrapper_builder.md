## mParivahan / VAHAN Data Acquisition & Intelligence Expansion Framework

### Core Strategic Role

mParivahan / VAHAN should serve as the foundational regulatory intelligence layer of the automotive data ecosystem, providing authoritative, compliance-grade vehicle registration and legal operational data across India.
