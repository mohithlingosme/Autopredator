Overview

Autopredator’s vehicle maintenance module enables complete lifecycle management of every fleet asset—from preventive checks and predictive alerts to cost tracking and compliance reporting. Integrate your service centers, schedule tasks, and stay ahead of failures.

Key Maintenance Features

Maintenance Scheduling: Schedule and automate service intervals by mileage, time, or engine hours.
Service History Logs: Record and retrieve digital maintenance records and invoices.
Predictive Maintenance: Prevent breakdowns with AI-powered failure predictions.
Tyre & Battery Management: Track health, replacements, and warranty details.
Brake, Oil & Suspension Monitoring: Manage essential component health with reminders.
Real-Time Health Diagnostics: Use OBD/IoT inputs for live fault codes and performance data.
Spare Part Forecasting: Plan inventory and vendor needs based on usage data.
Mobile Issue Reporting: Allow drivers to log breakdowns with images via mobile app.

Compliance & Workshop Integration

Emission & FC Reminders: Never miss PUC tests or fitness renewals again.
AMC & Warranty Alerts: Track AMCs and manufacturer warranties for each component.
Service Center APIs: Integrate seamlessly with authorized garages for status updates and digital job cards.
RTO Check Integration: Track vehicle health in sync with government records.
