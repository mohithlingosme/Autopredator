# AUTOPREDATOR – Full Ecosystem App Map

> Master list of apps and sub-products you can build under the AUTOPREDATOR brand. You don’t need to build everything at once – this is your long-term roadmap.

---

## 1. Core Foundation Apps (Start Here)

1. **Autopredator Hub (Main Website + Account App)**
   Central landing portal + authentication + navigation to all sub‑apps.

   * Brand site, about, blog, FAQs
   * User registration / login / KYC
   * Unified dashboard showing all vehicles, alerts, and linked apps

2. **Vehicle Vault – Documents & Compliance Manager**
   Single place to store and track all vehicle-related documents.

   * RC, insurance, PUC, fitness, permits, challans
   * Renewal reminders (SMS, WhatsApp, email)
   * State‑wise compliance rules, penalty calculator

3. **Garage & Maintenance Manager**
   Service history + upcoming service planner.

   * Next service km/date prediction
   * Job cards, parts used, labour cost history
   * Recommended service packages by OEM / usage

4. **Fuel & Expense Tracker**
   Track cost of ownership for each vehicle.

   * Fuel entry log, mileage analytics
   * Toll, parking, fines, miscellaneous expenses
   * Cost per km, cost per trip, per‑month report

5. **Mini Fleet Manager (1–10 Vehicles)**
   For families, small taxi owners, farmers, small businesses.

   * Simple dashboard for all vehicles
   * Compliance and service alerts
   * Basic driver allocation and trip log

## 2. Ownership, Compliance & Legal Apps

6. **LegalDesk – Vehicle Legal & RTO Helper**

   * Challan tracking and basic dispute guidance
   * Permit types, tax slabs, overloading rules
   * Drafts for simple representations / letters (non‑legal advice disclaimer)

7. **Permit & Fitness Tracker**

   * All India permit, state permits, tourist permits
   * Fitness certificate reminders and workflows
   * Suggested nearby RTO agents / test centers

8. **Challan Center – Traffic Fines & Violations App**

   * Fetch challans by vehicle/RC number (where APIs available)
   * Breakdown by offence type, location, date
   * Payment links + dispute/explanation notes

9. **Ownership Transfer & Re‑Registration Helper**

   * Step‑by‑step guide for RC transfer, NOC, hypothecation removal
   * Forms checklist, fee estimate, timeline tracker
   * Connect to verified agents (commission model)

10. **Cross‑Border Operations Assistant (State Tax & Entry)**

* For trucks, buses, taxis crossing states
* Road tax, entry permits, checkpost compliance
* Pre‑trip checklist and cost estimation

## 7. AI, Analytics & Intelligence Apps

30. **Autopredator Insight – Analytics & BI Dashboard (B2B)**

* Cross‑fleet analytics for large operators, OEMs, insurers
* Risk scores, cost per km, utilisation heatmaps
* PDF / PPT report exports

31. **AI Vehicle Advisor (What Should I Buy?)**

* Chat wizard: use case → budget → fuel → region → recommendation
* Compare TCO, resale, mileage, service network
* Suggest new vs used, ICE vs EV, segment comparisons

32. **AI Mechanic – Symptom to Possible Issue**

* User describes sound/feel/symptom
* Suggest possible issues and urgency level (visit now / later)
* Educate user to avoid overcharging and fraud (not a replacement for real mechanic)

33. **AI Route & Schedule Optimizer (For Fleets)**

* Reduce fuel and time by smart routing
* Assign trips to best‑placed vehicles
* Consider driver duty hours and restrictions

34. **Risk & Safety Score Engine**

* Combines driving behaviour, maintenance, age of vehicle, accident history
* Generates RN (risk number) for fleets, insurers, lenders
* Input to premium pricing and loan eligibility

35. **Predictive Maintenance Engine**

* Uses past breakdowns, usage patterns, and conditions
* Predicts part failure risk and suggests preventive replacement
* Integrates with Garage & Fleet apps

### How to Use This App Map

* **MVP Suggestion (First Wave):**
  Start with: Autopredator Hub, Vehicle Vault, Garage & Maintenance Manager, Fuel & Expense Tracker, and AutoMart.

* **Second Wave:**
  Add: Finance Desk, InsurePro, Mini Fleet Manager, Workshop Portal, EV Center.

* **Later Scaling:**
  Gradually bring in AI apps (AI Vehicle Advisor, AI Mechanic, Analytics) and specialised verticals (AgriDrive, BuildDrive, SchoolBus Manager, etc.).

You can now pick any subset (e.g., 5–10 apps) and we can create **detailed PRD + folder structure + tech stack + prompts for AI coders** for each one.
